Ollama Cookie Sync OPTIONAL

This extension is optional. The Hermes ollama-usage-monitor plugin works fully without it — it only auto-refreshes your cookie so you don't have to re-paste it manually (~every 2 months).

The local listener that writes the cookie into ~/.hermes/ollama_cookie.txt.
Printed once when you start the listener, or in ~/.hermes/ollama-cookie-sync-token.txt.
